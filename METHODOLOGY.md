# Research Methodology

SOLMEME.VIP monitors rapidly changing Solana meme token conditions using real-time and short observation-window market data.

Research categories may include:

- Market structure
- Persistence of activity
- Liquidity conditions
- Tradability
- Buyer and seller activity
- Holder concentration
- Momentum changes
- Risk signals
- Short-window outcomes

## Watch Classification

Watch and Strong Watch labels describe observed market conditions detected by the platform.

These labels:

- Are research classifications
- Do not represent guaranteed outcomes
- Are not instructions to purchase or sell a token
- May change quickly as market conditions evolve

## Observation Process

At a high level, SOLMEME.VIP:

1. Detects newly launched Solana meme tokens
2. Collects available market and structural observations
3. Identifies developing Watch conditions
4. Monitors whether conditions strengthen, remain stable, or weaken
5. Displays risk signals and short observation-window results

## Data Limitations

Newly launched meme tokens may have incomplete, delayed, inconsistent, or rapidly changing market data.

Missing data, low liquidity, concentration risk, sudden selling pressure, and extreme volatility may affect the reliability of any observation.

## Proprietary Research

Core formulas, private thresholds, infrastructure details, automated systems, and proprietary strategy logic are not publicly disclosed.

SOLMEME.VIP publishes research classifications and risk observations without exposing its private detection systems.

All classifications, scores, labels, and observations are provided for research and informational purposes only.

They do not constitute financial advice, an investment recommendation, a trading signal, or a guarantee of future performance.
